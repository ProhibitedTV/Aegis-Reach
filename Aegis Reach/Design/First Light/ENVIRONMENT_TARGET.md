# FIRST LIGHT // Environment Target

First Light should read as an intentional place before the HUD names it.

## Core visual rule

The player should understand **where they are, where the human route continues, and what happened here** from terrain, silhouettes, lighting, infrastructure and clutter before reading a floating objective label.

The target is not maximum prop count. It is layered, readable composition:

1. **Terrain establishes the place.** Vesper is a receded hypersaline basin with pale evaporite floor, orange-brown shelves, dark basalt, old tide history and a continuous engineered road cut through it.
2. **Landmarks establish direction.** Survey Camp 12, Gate 07, Northstar, Operations and AEGIS must each have a distinct long-distance silhouette.
3. **Infrastructure links the spaces.** Route beacons, utility pylons, work lights, barriers and graded shoulders visually connect one named area to the next.
4. **Props explain human use.** Crates, cots, generators, desks, oxygen bottles and emergency staging appear in coherent work zones instead of being sprinkled randomly.
5. **Lighting explains function.** Meridian civilian areas use warm practical work light; military thresholds and AEGIS use colder cyan; Northstar uses warmer industrial light; Choir-adjacent spaces become stranger without becoming unreadably dark.
6. **Built geometry is embedded into terrain.** Do not place large rooms on top of the landscape like detached boxes. Native terrain owns grounded walking surfaces; modular geometry provides walls, roofs, portals, machinery, catwalks and elevated decks.
7. **No coplanar generated floors.** The previous custom floor slabs z-fought with MAX native terrain and are prohibited until a proven MAX-native floor solution exists.

## Space identities

### Meridian Shelf / insertion
A wide, quiet alien landscape. The player should immediately see signs that a human corridor exists ahead: isolated utility infrastructure, distant work lights and a readable descent toward Camp 12.

### Survey Camp 12
A real civilian survey site, not a label over props. Strong silhouette from a survey mast and paired habitat modules. Work zones include shelter, science equipment, generator/cargo, evacuation staging and the missing-person clue. The road visibly leaves the camp.

### Dry Tide Channel
Compression after the camp. Native terrain does most of the work. Sparse geology, old survey markers and a utility spine maintain route confidence without turning the basin into a prop corridor.

### Gate 07
The first monumental threshold. It should visibly swallow the civilian road into a military retrofit embedded in the shelf. Large buttresses, a clear opening, cold light and asymmetric occupation clutter. The player should recognize it from hundreds of meters away.

### Northstar
Industrial cathedral. Twin vertical stacks, large machinery, amber light, maintenance lanes and a clear alternate flank. It should be identifiable by silhouette before the objective text appears.

### Operations
Human scale. Lower roof, recognizable entrance, desks, records, lockers, emergency cots and evacuation evidence. It must feel like people worked here before the Wardens requisitioned it.

### AEGIS Excavation
Second monumental reveal. Clean military geometry becomes larger and more vertical around the excavation. Cyan-edged arrival frame and gallery lead to the darker exposed Choir object. The player should experience compression, then a dramatic geological/architectural release.

## Composition quality gate

Before calling an environment pass complete, capture native MAX screenshots with HUD ignored and ask:

- Can I identify the location without its floating name?
- Is the intended route obvious from composition alone?
- Is there one dominant landmark instead of many equal-weight shapes?
- Do props form believable functional clusters?
- Are there any giant repeated black frames, box grids, floating props, intersections or z-fighting surfaces?
- Do structures look embedded into the terrain?
- Can I look backward and understand where I came from?
- Does the next major space tease itself before I reach it?

The aspirational screenshot language is: **classic sci-fi FPS readability + believable industrial/civilian world history + Vesper's unique salt-basin geology**. Do not copy another game's assets or architecture; copy the discipline of readable spatial storytelling.
