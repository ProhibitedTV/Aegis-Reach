# Survey Camp 12 — Sheltered Outpost Target

Camp 12 should feel like a place a small Meridian survey team chose because the geography protects them, not a collection of props placed on a flat pad.

## Spatial idea

- The camp occupies a shallow impact/weathering hollow in the old salt shelf.
- A rock crescent protects the west and north sides.
- A cave-like rock recess/overhang on the west side contains the field lab and survey equipment.
- The southeast edge opens naturally into the graded service road toward Gate 07.
- The player should understand the exit route without reading HUD text.

## Three readable layers

### 1. Human / cozy core
Cluster familiar objects tightly in the sheltered lee: tents, picnic table, bench/chair, lantern, radio, sleeping bag, water cooler, stove and coffee pot. This is where the missing survey crew actually lived between shifts.

### 2. Survey / high-tech recess
Use construction survey stands, cable reels and a compact sci-fi field-lab facade embedded into the rock-cut side. Blue/cyan technical accents belong here, not everywhere in the camp.

### 3. Logistics / security perimeter
Place containers, generator, portable water, crates, barriers, oxygen bottles and military supplies on the exposed eastern/southern edge. This layer should feel functional and slightly improvised rather than cozy.

## Composition rules

- Geography is the largest landmark; props support it.
- Do not ring the camp with identical poles or giant frames.
- Keep the center visually intimate and relatively low.
- Put tall silhouettes on the crater rim or rock wall, not between the player and the camp.
- Keep the road opening free of clutter.
- No generated coplanar floor meshes; native terrain remains the walking surface.
- Installed licensed assets are referenced from the user's MAX entitybank and are never copied into Git.
- Camp 12 should communicate 'remote survey outpost under military pressure' before the player reads its name.
