# VESPER BRINE SHELF // PLAYTEST CHECKLIST

This pass is intentionally a greybox-plus story layer. Do not judge final art density yet. Judge whether the open space deserves to become part of the critical path.

## 1. Can the player physically reach it?

From insertion, turn toward the south perimeter.

Expected:
- a broad central breach replaces four old bastion-wall segments;
- a shallow apron bridges the fortress deck to the exterior shelf;
- the player can cross without jumping or snagging;
- no invisible collision wall blocks the opening.

Failure conditions:
- falling through the shelf;
- getting stuck on the apron seam;
- wall collision remaining after visible wall removal;
- shelf collision causing huge frame spikes.

## 2. Does it feel open?

Stand near the center of the shelf and rotate 360 degrees.

The intended read:
- fortress behind you;
- Aurelia / orbital infrastructure above and beyond;
- sparse ridge masses, not a clutter maze;
- at least two obvious movement choices around the ridges;
- distant vents / survey equipment giving scale.

If every interesting object is equally loud, reduce props.

## 3. Does the vanished sea read without dialogue?

Before approaching a story prop, look for:
- pale ground versus dark basalt;
- repeated horizontal mineral line on the fortress exterior;
- same pale mineral band on old tide gauges;
- old equipment sitting at elevations that imply a former shoreline/shelf.

Ask one question after looking around for 20 seconds:

> Would a player plausibly conclude that water/brine used to occupy this basin even if Kestrel never spoke?

If no, the environmental rule is not visually consistent enough yet.

## 4. Human history

Walk to:
- `WORLD // MIRA SURVEY WRECK`
- `WORLD // TIDE GAUGE 17`
- `WORLD // TIDE GAUGE 22`

Expected:
- Kestrel reacts only when combat is quiet;
- each line is short;
- the object already communicates enough that the line feels like confirmation, not explanation;
- lines fire once per session.

If the chatter feels intrusive, increase trigger proximity or remove a line entirely.

## 5. Combat potential

Do not add enemies to the shelf yet. Instead, imagine a fight there.

Check whether the terrain naturally supports:
- a long-range opening angle;
- a ridge flank on either side;
- a dangerous open crossing;
- one strong elevated position;
- retreat routes that do not simply reverse into a dead end.

If the answer is no, change terrain before placing enemies.

## 6. Scale

The shelf is roughly one fortress-width wide and extends several thousand MAX units beyond the gate.

It should feel like the **edge of a much larger basin**, not a courtyard added behind the level.

Use screenshots from:
1. fortress looking outward;
2. far shelf looking back at Relayfall;
3. tide gauge with fort and Aurelia in the background;
4. survey wreck looking toward the breach.

Those four views will tell us more than close-up prop screenshots.

## Promotion gate

Only after this pass succeeds should we:
- move the player spawn onto the exterior shelf;
- retarget the CineGuru cold open to begin over the basin;
- add the first outdoor enemy pocket;
- replace greybox ridge/fortress pieces with selected Offworld/Cyber assets;
- add drifting salt particles, vent steam, and surface decals;
- introduce a vehicle/crawler if the space proves large enough to justify one.
