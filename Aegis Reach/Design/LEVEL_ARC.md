# AEGIS REACH // CAMPAIGN ARC

The vertical slice should prove that Aegis Reach can move between open landscape, dense military architecture, and strange buried spaces without losing combat clarity.

## Mission 01 — RELAYFALL

**Fantasy:** breach an occupied defense relay and turn its orbital network back on.

**Emotional shape:** isolation -> competence -> suspicion.

### Beat 0: Vesper Brine Shelf

The player now begins outside the fortress on the exposed shelf.

No immediate firefight. Give the player 15–30 seconds to exist in the place.

What they see:
- Aurelia filling part of the sky;
- salt dust crossing the ground;
- the old floodline on Relayfall's exterior structures;
- broken survey masts and one overturned field shelter;
- the fortress sitting above the pale basin like a later structure dropped onto older geology;
- distant orbital-spine debris.

What they learn without dialogue:
- people worked here before soldiers occupied it;
- Vesper used to be wetter;
- the current installation is only one node in a much larger network.

Gameplay:
- broad open approach;
- optional left/right ridge lines;
- one small supply/story detour;
- no enemies until the player commits toward the breach.

### Beat 1: The Breach

The first two Wardens own the gate and force the player to move from open terrain into deliberate cover.

The fight should demonstrate:
- rifle-range readability;
- shield pressure and recovery;
- enemy role differences;
- freedom to approach from more than one angle.

### Beat 2: Northstar Yard

Military geometry overlays an older survey yard. The old brine line is visible on everything that predates AEGIS.

The player begins to notice circular survey markings around a fracture that has nothing to do with the relay objective.

### Beat 3: Lantern Crossfire

Warmer industrial area. Steam/geothermal service infrastructure becomes prominent.

Optional world detail:
- abandoned maintenance alcove with Mira Sen's waveform notation;
- a route that lets curious players flank the fight and discover story simultaneously.

### Beat 4: AEGIS Core

Tallest and most technologically oppressive space.

The core's interference modifies shield behavior.

After capture:
- lighting flips toward cyan;
- the orbital spine answers visually;
- a distant buried structure under the brine shelf briefly resonates or exposes a repeating light pattern;
- Kestrel reacts only after the player has seen it.

### Beat 5: Kestrel Run

Return through familiar spaces under different state lighting.

Extraction should feel faster and more confident than the entry.

The final image should include the open shelf and Aurelia again, now with active defense lights crossing the horizon.

## Mission 02 — WHITE MERIDIAN

**Fantasy:** cross the open moon to reach a silent survey station before the Wardens do.

**Emotional shape:** freedom -> awe -> vulnerability -> discovery.

This is the mission that proves Aegis Reach can do large outdoor combat.

### Terrain language

A broad evaporite basin cut by dark basalt ridges and old brine channels.

The player can read the route from kilometers away:
- a dead weather tower;
- a geothermal plume;
- the survey station on a distant terrace;
- Aurelia's position in the sky.

### Combat language

Do not fill the basin with enemies.

Use 3–4 authored pockets separated by traversal and observation:
- ridge ambush with long-range roles;
- abandoned crawler site with close combat around machinery;
- optional high shelf containing a useful weapon / story evidence;
- final approach where Wardens and a new sci-fi support enemy interact.

The open space should let the player choose when to start fights and from what distance.

### Signature detail

An old survey road crosses a dry brine channel on pylons. Every pylon has the same pale mineral line several meters above the current channel floor. One pylon has been replaced recently and lacks the stain entirely.

No dialogue calls this out.

## Mission 03 — UNDERTIDE

**Fantasy:** descend below the vanished sea floor into the infrastructure everyone has been lying about.

**Emotional shape:** curiosity -> unease -> wonder -> threat.

The first clearly nonhuman environment.

The architecture must not feel like generic "ancient alien temple."

The Choir is embedded into geology:
- conductive ribs emerge from basalt as if the rock grew around them;
- mineral veins align to impossible repeating intervals;
- spaces are partly natural fracture and partly engineered resonance chamber;
- water/mineral history remains visible even underground.

Combat becomes tighter and stranger, with vertical threats and environmental state changes.

---

## Mission 04 — AURELIA'S SHADOW

**Fantasy:** fight across an orbital tether / defense platform during a planetary shadow transit.

**Emotional shape:** scale -> urgency -> sacrifice.

This is where the player finally understands why AEGIS exists and why the Wardens were willing to seize it.

# Campaign pacing doctrine

The original combat ideal is not constant intensity. Each mission should have a recognizable waveform:

1. **arrive somewhere worth seeing**;
2. **understand the space**;
3. **choose how to enter pressure**;
4. **improvise inside the sandbox**;
5. **earn a quiet release**;
6. **notice something that changes the player's understanding**;
7. **repeat with escalation**.

The world and combat should continuously trade responsibility for the player's attention.
