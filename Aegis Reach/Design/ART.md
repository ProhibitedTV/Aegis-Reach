# Aegis Reach title artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then copied unchanged into this project at `Files\imagebank\aegis_reach\title-art.png`. Used as the native storyboard screen backdrop. It is concept art, not a gameplay screenshot. Menu typography remains editable in the storyboard.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: widescreen 16:9 background art for the original sci-fi infantry game Aegis Reach. Create a polished atmospheric painted science fiction military game title background. A fortified remote relay outpost at blue twilight, angular gunmetal blast walls, geometric cyan signal pylons, amber warning lights, a giant broken antenna arch in the distance, hazy distant alien mountains. A lone dark navy armored infantry operative with a compact rifle stands on the far right, viewed from behind, facing the outpost. Grounded hard-surface technology, crisp cinematic art, teal and amber accents, high detail but restrained composition. The left 45 percent is a dark quiet area of sky and mist reserved for title and menu typography. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks or UI. Original world and armor design, no Halo characters or insignia, no ringworld. 1920x1080 landscape.

Modular station meshes and the panel texture atlas were generated procedurally with the original `tools/build_game.py` script. The tactical diagram is generated from actual map placements by `tools/brand_project.py`.
